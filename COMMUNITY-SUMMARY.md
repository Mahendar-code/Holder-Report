# Community summary: RBNT holder report, Q3 2026

Not financial advice. Every number comes from the full report: https://redbelly-onchain-holder.vercel.app

## A few paragraphs

The first quarterly RBNT holder report covers 1 July to 30 September 2026. Every number is read from the Redbelly chain at the last block before midnight UTC on 30 June and on 30 September. It describes trends in groups and names only wallets that are already public. It is not financial advice and makes no price forecast.

Staking grew over the quarter. RBNT staked in the two Reddex pools rose from 635.8 million to 751.3 million. At the same time the supply of Wrapped RBNT fell from 1.82 billion to 1.74 billion, with the RBNT backing it falling by the same amount.

Most RBNT still sits in Redbelly's reserved wallets: 59.5% at the end of the quarter. The largest movements were between those wallets: 500 million from Ecosystem (Locked) to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked), 253.1 million from there to the Investors wallet, and 33.3 million from Governance (Locked) to Governance (Unlocked). Ecosystem (Unlocked) was the only reserved wallet that sent RBNT outside the group, including 15 million to the staking pools for rewards.

Outside the named wallets, 47,022 addresses held 1 RBNT or more at the end of the quarter, up from 46,862, and the largest 100 held 87.3% of community holdings. Routescan labels no exchange wallet on Redbelly yet, so the report shows no exchange flows rather than guess. The full report, the data and the method are at https://redbelly-onchain-holder.vercel.app.
